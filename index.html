DL architecture explorers

Interactive pages that show what the boxes in an architecture diagram actually compute.

  • CNN, 2D — convolution step by step, ReLU, pooling, channels, receptive field, to a prediction.
  • CNN, 3D — the same network as H×W×C blocks, with kernel sweeps and receptive-field cones.
  • CNN, training — real digits stream in; one step in full (forward, loss, backward, update), then the arc of many: curves, kernels learning edges, accuracy on held-out digits. Trained weights load into the other two pages.

Planned: ViT, ConvNeXt, CoAtNet.
