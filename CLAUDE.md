# DL architecture explorers — project conventions

Purpose: interactive, visual explanations of how DL architectures compute (CNN and ViT built, each with an explorer and a training page; then U-Net, CoAtNet, ConvNeXt, in that order). The audience is someone who uses these models daily and wants the concrete steps behind diagrams like "Conv 3×3 → ReLU → MaxPool → … → FC".

## Layout
- `core/` — pure JS model code and shared presets (ES modules, no DOM). Tests: `node core/cnn.test.mjs`, `node core/data.test.mjs`, `node core/train.test.mjs`, `node core/vit.test.mjs`, `node core/race.test.mjs`. `core/race.js` steps the CNN and the ViT on the same digits, for both the offline comparison and the live race.
- `ui/` — shared DOM helpers: `terms.js` (term lists, one per page family: `TERMS` for the CNN pages, `VIT_TERMS` for the ViT explorer, `VIT_TRAIN_TERMS` for the ViT training page; tooltip, glossary) and `terms.css`; `grid.js` (canvas grids, hit-testing, colours, and the graded green `glow` and yellow `win` the ViT page uses); `trained.js` (trained-weights hand-off through local storage, a key for the CNN and one for the ViT); `vitline.js` and `vitline.css` (the ViT pages' tensor drawing: views, lines of one or several rows, arcs, paint); `blocks3d.js` (the 3D block renderer: scene, instanced cubes, highlights, labels, orbit camera, picking). ES modules, imported by the pages.
- `data/` — the 16×16 MNIST subset (`mnist16.bin`, format and licence in its README); `tools/` — the standard-library Python script that makes it, `train_vit.mjs`, which trains the ViT and writes `models/vit16.json`, and `vit_vs_cnn.mjs`, which measures both models on 100 to 3,000 digits and writes `models/vit_vs_cnn.json`; `docs/superpowers/` — design specs and implementation plans.
- `index.html` — the landing page: the colour grammar, then one section per architecture (schematic, a plain definition, its pages as cards, or "what the page will show" while planned). When an architecture gets pages, turn its section's list into cards and its badge into the page count.
- `cnn-2d/`, `cnn-3d/`, `cnn-train/`, `vit/`, `vit-train/` — one self-contained page each, with a breadcrumb back to the landing page. New pages follow the same pattern (`unet/`, `coatnet/`, `convnext/`). `vit/` builds its pinned overview line and its five sections with one column builder: a section is the overview's columns at a larger cell size, and every copy of a tensor lights together on hover.
- `models/` — `vit16.json`, the ViT explorer's weights, made by `node tools/train_vit.mjs` (the command is in its `meta.made`); `vit_vs_cnn.json`, the measured comparison on the ViT training page, made by `node tools/vit_vs_cnn.mjs`.
- No build step. Plain HTML + ES modules served statically (GitHub Pages workflow in `.github/workflows/pages.yml`). Local dev: `python3 -m http.server 8000` from the repo root; modules need http, not file://.
- External scripts only from cdnjs or jsDelivr, pinned to exact versions. three.js is r128 UMD from cdnjs; OrbitControls is not in that build, so `ui/blocks3d.js` has its own small orbit implementation, used by the 3D explorer and the training page's 3D view.

## Design rules (keep these)
- Visual and interactive over tables and text. The owner explicitly removed a table; don't reintroduce one.
- Linked highlighting across views is the core interaction. Yellow = forward kernel window; green = what a value is computed from: an activation's receptive field (upstream), a gradient's sources (downstream, up to the loss), a weight gradient's two maps, and on the training page the step of the backward pass just taken. On the ViT training page a gradient's green runs downstream and, through attention, is graded by a column of the map (the queries that read a token), the mirror of the forward row. After attention (ViT page) green is graded: the hovered token's own patch full, every other token by its attention weight, scaled so the most-attended reaches 0.9. Keep the two colours distinct and consistent across every page.
- Weights sit where they act: between the map they read and the map they make, marked as learned (tinted column in 2D, accent frame in 3D). Where training is shown, three rows: forward (activations and w), backward (each gradient under what it is the gradient of), update (w′ under each weight). This is the layout to reuse for the next architectures.
- Hovering a cell anywhere traces its receptive field through every upstream tensor. At the head (global average, classes) show only the direct link, not the full cone back to the input.
- Every technical term gets a dotted inline definition (tooltip) and an entry in a Terms section. The mechanism is shared: `ui/terms.js` (`TERMS` array, `installTerms()`) with `ui/terms.css`; every page imports it rather than carrying a copy.
- Weights: layer-1 kernels are hand-picked edge detectors; layer-2 and FC are random. Pages must say so wherever a prediction appears. The training page (`cnn-train/`) trains all three layers live; its weights can be loaded into the explorer pages, which then say so in a banner and in their wording. The ViT page runs on weights trained offline (`models/vit16.json`), can switch to random ones, and says which, with the held-out accuracy, wherever a prediction appears.
- Theme-aware (light/dark via CSS tokens), responsive, reduced-motion respected. Canvas/WebGL elements must be sized with CSS as well as pixels (a 2× DPR overflow bug has already bitten once).
